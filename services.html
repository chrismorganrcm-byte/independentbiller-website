Our services

Revenue cycle support for your practice.

Choose the support that fits your workflow, with options to coordinate multiple parts of the billing process.

01

Complete RCM & medical billing

Support for the billing lifecycle, from intake-related checks and claim workflow through payment tracking and follow-up.

02

Eligibility verification

Eligibility and benefits checks to help identify coverage details and potential issues before services are billed.

03

Claim preparation & submission

Support with claim preparation, submission workflow, claim status tracking and correcting issues identified during processing.

04

Payment posting

Post insurance and patient payments based on remittance and payment information provided through approved workflows.

05

AR follow-up & patient statements

Follow up on outstanding receivables and support the preparation and management of patient statements under your practice’s policies.

06

Provider credentialing

Credentialing and enrollment coordination for Medicare, Medicaid and commercial payers. Timelines and approval decisions are controlled by the respective payer.

07

EDI, ERA & EFT enrollment assistance

Assistance coordinating electronic data interchange, electronic remittance advice and electronic funds transfer enrollment. Our service assistance is offered at no additional service fee; third-party charges may apply.

08

Payer rate negotiation support

Administrative support to help prepare for payer rate discussions and organize relevant practice information. Outcomes are not guaranteed; payer decisions remain with the payer.
