About IndependentBiller

Support for the business side of healthcare.

Our focus is helping independent practices navigate billing and administrative workflows with a clear, service-oriented approach.

Our approach

Organized work. Clear communication.

Medical billing involves many connected tasks. IndependentBiller is designed to support practices with day-to-day revenue cycle activities, including eligibility checks, claim workflows, payment posting, accounts receivable follow-up and credentialing coordination.

We aim to be a practical extension of your administrative workflow, with service scope and responsibilities agreed in advance.
