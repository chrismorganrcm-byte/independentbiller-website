Privacy notice

How general business inquiries through this website are handled.

Information submitted through forms

If you submit a website inquiry, the information you provide may be transmitted through FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email service, to IndependentBiller at chris@independentbiller.com. The service provider may process technical information as part of delivering the form. Review the provider’s terms and privacy information before activating the form.

Do not submit health information

This website is intended for general business inquiries, not the transfer of patient information or protected health information (PHI). Do not submit patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, insurance identifiers, clinical details, claims, or other sensitive information through the forms or ordinary email.

Use of inquiry information

Information you submit may be used to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential services. Do not use the website to send confidential information or documents.

Third-party services and updates

This notice is a basic website template, not legal advice or a complete privacy compliance assessment. Review it with qualified counsel and update it to reflect your actual practices, applicable laws, vendors and retention policies before launch.
